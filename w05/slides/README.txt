Open index.html in Firefox or Chrome, then choose a video (Video_5.1.html ... Video_5.11.html, eleven videos, and one live page).

KEYS
  Right, Space     next step or slide          Left      back one step
  F                full screen                 ?         the list of keys
  P (or ح)         pen over the slide: draw on the slide; P again or Esc goes back to the same step
  W (or ص)         whiteboard: a white sheet you can draw on; W again or Esc goes back to the same step
                   With P or W on: 1 2 3 navy, coral, ink pen · 4 highlighter · L (م) laser · E (ث) eraser
                   Z (ئ) or Ctrl+Z undo · X (ء) clear this layer · T (ف) hide or show the pen tray (for recording)
                   Drawings are kept per slide until X or a reload. While a pen is on, the deck does not move.
  D (or ي)         derivation window, on slides with a "how we get this" button (or click the button):
                   click or Right: next line; Left: back; Esc, D or ي, or Right at the last line: back to the same slide and step
  C (or ؤ)         calculation window, on slides with an "= how?" button next to a number (or click the button):
                   how that number was computed, one step per click; Esc, C or ؤ, or Right at the last step: back to the same step
  ] / [            next / previous video             H (or ا)   back to index.html, the list of videos
  R (or ق)         recording mode on / off (see below)

STUDENT MODE AND RECORDING MODE (the rule)
  Student mode is the default. A small pill at the bottom left shows "slide 3/5 · step 2 of 4" with 44 px buttons
  (‹ back, › next); a click or tap on the right 40 % of the slide goes on, on the left 20 % goes back. The pill and the
  video buttons at the top right fade after 2.5 s without the mouse and come back when it moves.
  Recording mode shows only the slide: no pill, no slide counter, no video buttons, no whiteboard button, no hint.
  It is on when
    - the address ends in ?rec (for example Video_5.3.html?rec), or ?cam (which also draws the camera circle), or
    - R was pressed on this computer (it is remembered for all Week 5 videos until R is pressed again).
  The pen tray stays visible in recording mode while a pen is on; T hides it. Nothing is ever drawn in the camera circle
  (bottom right): the pill sits bottom left, the pen tray bottom left.

Each click plays one step: the mark first, then its value, then the sentence. Going back shows the step at once.
Live slides: click inside to use the page. PageDown, N or Space go on; arrows move a slider; P, W, R, ], [, H, F also work there;
Esc gives the keys back to the slides. The live page on its own has a "Week 5" link back to index.html.
  5.5: Where are the boundaries?
For the instructor: add ?cam=1 to the address (before #) to see the circle kept clear for the camera (also over the windows).
Presenter mode (instructor only): Video_5.k.html?present shows the instructor's script over the slides and opens a second,
clean window (?rec&mirror) that follows it exactly, for OBS. It needs the instructor folder next to this one; without it,
?present shows a short notice and the deck works as usual. Q (or ض) hides or shows the script panel.
Recording safety: reveal's slide overview (Esc/O), black screen (B, .), jump (G) and speaker view (S) are switched off.
