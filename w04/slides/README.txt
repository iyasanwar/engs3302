Week 4 slides, ENGS 3302 Pattern Recognition (reveal.js 5.1.0, MIT licence). No internet needed.

Open index.html in Firefox or Chrome, then choose a video (Video_4.1.html ... Video_4.5.html).
  F                full screen          Esc / O   all slides
  Right, Space     next step or slide   Left      back one step
  S                speaker notes window (allow the pop-up)
  W (or ص)         whiteboard: a white sheet over the slide; W, ص or Esc again returns to the same step
  D (or ي)         derivation window, on slides with a "how we get this" button (or click the button):
                   click or Right: next line; Left: back; Esc, D or ي: back to the same slide and step
Each click plays one step: numbers move to where they are used and the result settles. Going back shows the step at once.
Live slides: click inside to use the page. PageDown, N or Space go on; arrows move a slider.
