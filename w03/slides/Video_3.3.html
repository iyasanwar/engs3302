Three fish, each a sea bass with probability 0.6

Sea bass in a net of three fish

A random variable is a number that comes out of an experiment. It is discrete if it takes separate values only, like 0, 1, 2, 3.

Three fish in a net. Each is a sea bass with probability 0.6, independent of the others. X = the number of sea bass.

One order, BBS (independent: multiply):

0.6×0.6×0.4=0.144

Two sea bass come in 3 orders:

3×0.144=0.432= P(X = 2)

The probability of each value is the PMF, the probability mass function.

X is the number of sea bass in a net of three. Click 1: one order, sea bass, sea bass, salmon: 0.6 × 0.6 × 0.4 = 0.144 (multiply, because the fish are independent). Click 2: two sea bass come in three orders: BBS, BSB, SBB: 3 × 0.144 = 0.432. Click 3: the orders become the PMF: 0.064, 0.288, 0.432, 0.216.

Two rules

Does the PMF add up to 1?

every P(X = x) ≥ 0all of them add up to 1

Stack the four probabilities: 0.064 + 0.288 + 0.432 + 0.216 = 1 exactly.

Forget that two sea bass come in 3 orders (and one in 3): the stack reaches only 0.52. If a PMF does not add up to 1, something is missing.

A PMF has two rules: no probability is negative, and they add up to 1. Click 1: stack them: exactly 1. Click 2: the check is not a formality. Forget the three orders and you get 0.064, 0.096, 0.144, 0.216: only 0.52. If the sum is not 1, something is missing.

The average of a random variable

The expected value

E[X] = Σ x · P(X = x)multiply each value by its probability, and add

average of 100,000 nets: 1.798

0+0.288+0.864+0.648=1.8

1.8 is the balance point of the bars. It is also 3 × 0.6: three fish, each a sea bass with probability 0.6.

No net holds 1.8 sea bass. E[X] is the average over many nets: 100,000 simulated nets give 1.798.

E[X]: multiply each value by its probability and add (the Σ sign, Math Toolkit). Click 1: 0 + 0.288 + 0.864 + 0.648 = 1.8. Click 2: the balance point of the bars, and 3 × 0.6. Click 3: no net holds 1.8; it is the average over many nets: 100,000 simulated nets give 1.798 (the lab does this). Next video: lightness and width are not whole numbers.
