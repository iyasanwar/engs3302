Change one thing only: the spread of both classes. Same means, same priors.

σ = 2: the halfway point stays, but the shift is 4 times larger, because σ² went from 1 to 4.

Why? Wide curves overlap a lot. Then the measurement tells us little. So the prior decides more.

Thresholds

  1. x* = 5.5 + ⅓ ln(0.7/0.3) = 5.782: right of 5.5, because salmon is now the common class.
