So far: the same σ, so x² cancelled. Now salmon stays narrow, N(4, 1²). What if sea bass varies more?

Sea bass: N(7, 2²). Its curve is wide and low, with thick tails.

The x² terms no longer cancel. The scores cross twice: at 0.82 and at 5.18.

A very dark fish, x = 0.5: sea bass wins, −6.49 against −7.04. The narrow salmon tail falls faster.

Different spreads: two boundaries or none. Only equal spreads give exactly one.
