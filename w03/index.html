Density height = count ÷ (number of samples × bin width), so the area of a bar is the fraction of samples in it. The normal curve uses the mean and the standard deviation (dividing by n) of the chosen samples. Data: Palmer Penguins (Horst, Hill & Gorman 2020), CC0.
