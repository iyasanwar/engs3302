Week 3 slides, ENGS 3302 Pattern Recognition (reveal.js 5.1.0, MIT licence). No internet needed.

Open index.html in Firefox or Chrome, then choose a video (Video_3.1.html ... Video_3.5.html).
  F                full screen          Esc / O   all slides
  Right, Space     next step or slide   Left      back one step
  S                speaker notes window (allow the pop-up)
Each click plays one step: numbers move to where they are used and the result settles. Going back shows the step at once.
Live slides: click inside to use the page. PageDown, N or Space go on; arrows move a slider.
  3.2: How common is sea bass?   3.4: How crowded, how many?   3.5: One curve or three?
For the instructor: add ?cam=1 to the address (before #) to see the circle kept clear for the camera.
