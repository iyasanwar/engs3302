Density height = count ÷ (number of samples × bin width), so the area of a bar is the fraction of samples in it. The normal curve uses the mean and the standard deviation (dividing by n) of the chosen samples. Measurements are recorded in steps (flipper 1 mm, bill 0.1 mm, mass 25 g), so the recorded values from a to b stand for the interval from a − half a step to b + half a step; the model uses that interval.
